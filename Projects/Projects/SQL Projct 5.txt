FINAL CHALLENGE — Sales Performance Report

Ab ek professional business report banao.

For every city return:

city
order_count
total_sales
average_order_value
minimum_order
maximum_order

Then create one additional column:

performance

Rules:

total_sales > 100000
        → Excellent

total_sales BETWEEN 50000 AND 100000
        → Good

total_sales < 50000
        → Needs Improvement

Then:

Highest total sales first.

query = SELECT
    city,
    COUNT(*) AS order_count,
    SUM(total_amount) AS total_sales,
    AVG(total_amount) AS average_order_value,
    MAX(total_amount) AS maximum_order,
    MIN(total_amount) AS minimum_order,
CASE
    WHEN SUM(total_amount) > 100000 THEN 'Excellent'
    WHEN SUM(total_amount) BETWEEN 50000 AND 100000 THEN 'Good'
    WHEN SUM(total_amount) < 50000 THEN 'Needs Improvement'
END AS performance
    FROM orders
GROUP BY city
ORDER BY total_sales DESC

;

results:
Lahore	7	116200	16600	30000	3200	Excellent
Karachi	6	106701	17783.5	35000	6500	Excellent
Rawalpindi	3	89000	29666.666666666668	60000	8500	Good
Islamabad	6	80800	13466.666666666668	22000	2800	Good
Faisalabad	5	74301	14860.2	25000	7800	Good
Multan	3	43000	14333.333333333332	20000	4500	Needs Improvement
