Final Challenge — Sales Investigation

Business manager asks:

"Mujhe un customers ke orders chahiye jinke naam mein a ya A aata ho, jo Lahore, Islamabad ya Faisalabad se hain, aur jin ki order value 8,000 se 20,000 ke beech hai. Cancelled orders nahi chahiye. Highest-value order pehle show karo."

Return:

order_id
customer_name
city
order_date
total_amount
status


QUERY = SELECT 
order_id, customer_name, city, total_amount, status, order_date
FROM orders
WHERE (customer_name LIKE '%a%'  OR customer_name LIKE '%A%') AND city IN ('Islamabad' , 'Faisalabad') AND (total_amount BETWEEN 8000 AND 20000) AND (status != 'Cancelled')
ORDER BY total_amount DESC
